I am a researcher and practitioner working at the intersection of geospatial information systems, satellite remote sensing, and disaster risk reduction. My work is grounded in regional planning practice in West Java, Indonesia, where geospatial methods translate directly into infrastructure and resilience decisions.

Most recently, I led the spatial analysis component of a regional drinking water supply system (SPAM) survey covering six sub-districts and approximately 150,000 tax-registered objects in Purwakarta — combining cadastral data, building activity records, and field surveys to inform service-area planning.

On the research side, I am currently revising a manuscript on solar irradiance forecasting for data-sparse tropical regions using a hybrid WRF–LSTM approach — bridging numerical weather prediction with deep learning to address forecasting gaps in West Java's renewable energy planning.

Beyond my professional work, I maintain an interest in historical cartography and geo-visual storytelling, and I write occasional notes on geospatial workflows, open data, and disaster preparedness.
