The map below is an interactive demonstration combining OpenStreetMap, topographic, and satellite base layers with sample disaster-hazard markers across West Java. Use the layer control to toggle hazards and basemaps. This is built with Leaflet.js — lightweight, open-source, and ideal for embedding in academic sites.

Sample data shown is illustrative — replace /assets/maps/sample.geojson with your own datasets to feature real research outputs.

Layers

  • · OSM standard basemap
  • · Esri World Imagery
  • · OpenTopoMap
  • · Sample hazard points

Tech stack

  • · Leaflet.js 1.9
  • · GeoJSON data format
  • · Tile providers (OSM, Esri)
  • · No backend required

Use cases

  • · Hazard exposure mapping
  • · Field survey results
  • · Service area visualisation
  • · Public-facing dashboards
